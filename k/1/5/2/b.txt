# K1.5.2 SpinCloud SpiNNaker

The hardware called SpiNNaker from the company SpinCloud is designed to efficiently calculate Neuromorphic problems.
The learning outcomes in this skill go into detail of how neuromorphic problems can be solved in hardware and it can be used with a demo program.

## Learning Outcomes

* Understand what neuromorphic computing is, and what sort of problems it can solve
* Differentiate to know different hardware systems used in neuromorphic computing
* Operate the novel neuromorphic computing platform SpiNNaker-2, and learn how to perform basic neuromorphic simulations with it
