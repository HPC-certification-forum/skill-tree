# QC1 Quantum Computing Principles

This skill group introduces learners to the practical and mathematical foundations of quantum computing. Starting with the representation of quantum information, learners explore how a single qubit is described mathematically and visualised using the Bloch sphere. They then study how single qubits are manipulated with quantum gates, including measurement, the no-cloning theorem, and basic single-qubit circuits. Finally, learners extend these concepts to multi-qubit systems, learning how qubits combine through tensor products, how multi-qubit gates operate, and how entanglement arises and influences computation, culminating in an understanding of the gate model and universal gate sets.

## Requirements

External: Basic linear algebra  
External: Introductory quantum mechanics (helpful but not required)
