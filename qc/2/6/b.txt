# QC2.6 Grover's Algorithm

This skill introduces Grover's algorithm, which provides a quadratic speedup for unstructured search problems compared with classical approaches. Learners examine how amplitude amplification and interference are combined to increase the probability of measuring a marked state. Emphasis is placed on executing the algorithm step by step and interpreting the measurement outcomes.

## Requirements

* [[skill-tree:qc:1:3:b]]
* [[skill-tree:qc:2:1:b]]

## Learning Outcomes

* Describe the problem addressed by Grover's algorithm and its classical query complexity.
* Explain how amplitude amplification and interference are used to increase the probability of the marked state.
* Execute Grover's algorithm step by step for a simple oracle and search space.
* Calculate and interpret the measurement outcomes to identify the marked state.
