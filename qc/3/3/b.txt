# QC3.3 Quantum Approximate Optimisation Algorithm (QAOA)

This skill introduces the Quantum Approximate Optimisation Algorithm (QAOA) as a hybrid quantum-classical algorithm for finding approximate solutions to combinatorial optimisation problems. Learners are provided with a conceptual explanation of the problem Hamiltonian as a cost function that encodes the optimisation objective. Emphasis is placed on executing the algorithm on small examples and interpreting the results without requiring prior physics knowledge.

## Requirements

* [[skill-tree:qc:3:1:b]]

## Learning Outcomes

* Describe the purpose of QAOA and its hybrid quantum-classical workflow.
* Explain, in conceptual terms, how the problem Hamiltonian encodes an optimisation objective.
* Explain how parameterised quantum circuits explore candidate solutions.
* Execute the QAOA algorithm step by step for a small example.
* Calculate and interpret objective values and measurement probabilities to identify approximate solutions.
