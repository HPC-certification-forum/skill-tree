# QC3 Variational and Hybrid Quantum Algorithms

This parent node provides a high-level overview of the Variational Algorithms branch. Learners are introduced to the conceptual framework of hybrid quantum-classical algorithms, which use parameterised quantum circuits and classical optimisation loops to solve problems such as optimisation and simulation on near-term quantum devices (NISQ). The parent node contextualises the branch, highlighting the strengths, limitations, and workflow of these algorithms before learners progress to specific skills such as VQE and QAOA.

## Requirements

External:
* Basic linear algebra  
* Introductory quantum mechanics (helpful but not required)

Internal:
* [[skill-tree:qc:1:3:b]]

## Learning Outcomes

* Describe the general workflow of variational and hybrid quantum-classical algorithms.
* Explain how parameterised quantum circuits and classical optimisation routines interact in this framework.
* Identify the advantages of variational algorithms for near-term quantum devices (NISQ).
* Recognise the limitations of these algorithms, including slow convergence, barren plateaus, and the absence of guaranteed quantum advantage.
* Understand the purpose and conceptual differences of representative variational algorithms e.g., VQE and QAOA.
