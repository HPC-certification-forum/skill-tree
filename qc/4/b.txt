# QC4 Quantum Programming

This skill group introduces quantum programming from a practical and applied perspective. Learners explore how quantum algorithms are expressed as executable programs using contemporary quantum software frameworks, and how abstract circuit models are translated into code that can be executed on simulators or quantum hardware.

Emphasis is placed on understanding common programming patterns, the structure of the quantum software stack, and the practical workflow of developing, executing, and interpreting quantum programs. The focus is on operational competence and conceptual understanding rather than framework-specific expertise.

## Learning Outcomes

* Describe the circuit-based programming model and the common components of a quantum software stack.
* Compare quantum programming frameworks based on their shared abstractions and execution workflows.
* Translate gate-model quantum algorithms into executable quantum programs.
* Construct and execute quantum circuits on simulator backends and interpret their measurement results.
* Identify and correct basic errors in quantum circuit construction and execution.
* Implement parameterised quantum circuits and hybrid quantum-classical optimisation loops.
* Compute and interpret expectation values used in variational algorithms.
* Distinguish the practical execution workflows of gate-model and variational quantum algorithms.
