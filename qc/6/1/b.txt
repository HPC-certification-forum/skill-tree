# QC6.1 Quantum Simulation on Classical HPC Systems

This skill introduces methods for simulating quantum computation on classical high-performance computing systems. Learners examine different quantum simulation strategies, including state-vector simulation, tensor-network approaches, and sampling-based methods, and how their computational cost scales with system size.

The focus is on understanding memory requirements, parallelisation strategies, and practical limits, rather than on simulator implementation details.

## Requirements

* [[skill-tree:qc:1:3:b]]

## Learning Outcomes

* Describe the purpose of quantum simulation in algorithm development and benchmarking.
* Compare different simulation approaches in terms of accuracy, scalability, and resource requirements.
* Explain why classical simulation becomes infeasible as quantum system size increases.
