# QC6.1 Quantum Simulation on Classical HPC Systems

This skill introduces methods for simulating quantum computation on classical high-performance computing systems. Learners examine different quantum simulation strategies, including state-vector simulation, tensor-network approaches, and sampling-based methods, and how their computational cost scales with system size.

The focus is on understanding memory requirements, parallelisation strategies, and practical limits, rather than on simulator implementation details.

## Requirements

* [[skill-tree:qc:1:3:b]]

## Learning Outcomes

* Describe the purpose of classical quantum simulation in algorithm development and benchmarking.
* Explain how the memory and computational requirements of state-vector simulation scale with the number of qubits.
* Explain how entanglement, network topology, and contraction order affect the efficiency of tensor-network simulation.
* Compare state-vector, tensor-network, and approximate simulation methods in terms of accuracy, scalability, and resource requirements.
* Identify classes of quantum circuits and problems for which the different simulation approaches are suitable.
