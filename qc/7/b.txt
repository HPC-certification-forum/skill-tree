# QC7 Quantum Annealing and Adiabatic Computing

This skill introduces quantum annealing and adiabatic quantum computing as alternative computational models to the gate-based (circuit) approach. Learners develop an intuitive understanding of computation expressed as energy minimisation, where solutions correspond to low-energy configurations of a system. The skill differentiates idealised adiabatic quantum computing as a theoretical model from practical quantum annealers as physical implementations, and situates both within the broader landscape of quantum computation.

External:
* Basic linear algebra  
* Introductory quantum mechanics (helpful but not required)

## Learning Outcomes

* Distinguish between gate-based quantum computing and annealing or adiabatic computational models at a conceptual level.
* Explain how optimisation problems can be represented as energy minimisation problems and addressed through adiabatic evolution.
* Identify problem classes commonly associated with quantum annealing, including combinatorial optimisation and sampling problems.
* Describe, at a high level, the distinction between ideal adiabatic quantum computing models and practical quantum annealing implementations.
* Explain how practical constraints, such as noise, limited control, and connectivity and embedding limitations, influence the capabilities and interpretation of results from quantum annealers.
