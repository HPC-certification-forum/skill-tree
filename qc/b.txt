# QC Quantum Computing

Quantum computing is an emerging paradigm that leverages principles of quantum mechanics to provide known computational speedups for certain classes of problems and may offer advantages for others.
This node introduces learners to the foundations of quantum information, quantum programming, and the integration of quantum resources into high-performance computing (HPC) workflows.
By covering theory, algorithms, hardware, and hybrid approaches, this branch prepares learners for both conceptual understanding and practical experimentation.

## Requirements

External:
* Basic linear algebra  
* Introductory quantum mechanics (helpful but not required)

## Learning Outcomes

* Describe the fundamental principles used to represent, manipulate, and measure quantum information.
* Explain how representative quantum algorithms and protocols use quantum phenomena to address computational problems.
* Distinguish between gate-based, variational and hybrid, quantum-inspired, and annealing or adiabatic approaches to computation.
* Construct and execute simple quantum programs and interpret their results.
* Explain how quantum hardware, compilation, noise, error mitigation, and error correction affect the execution of quantum computations.
* Compare classical quantum-simulation approaches in terms of their scalability, accuracy, and resource requirements.
* Analyse how quantum processors and simulators can be integrated into hybrid quantum–HPC workflows.
* Assess, at a high level, the suitability, potential benefits, and current limitations of quantum-computing approaches relative to appropriate classical methods.
