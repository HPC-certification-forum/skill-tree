# SD1.2.2.5 Parallisms with Language extension

Deploy different programming models and language extensions in order to parallelize algorithms.
This includes SYCL, and OpenACC, and they should be compared other concepts like processes, vectorization, and threading.

## Requirements

## Learning Outcomes

* Assess the applicability of parallel language extensions like CUDA as well as their interoperability (e.g. combining OpenACC and CUDA).
* Understand Language Extensions and remember 
    * SYCL
    * OpenACC
* Compare the language extension to other concepts, such as processes, vectorizations, and Threading with OpenMP and Pthreads
